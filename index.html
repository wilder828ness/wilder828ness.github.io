Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns on New, unopened merchandise purchased from us within 30 days of you receiving it. No returns are accepted for damaged merchandise or packaging. We can't promise your item will arrive from its logistics journey in Mint condition, but we'll do everything we can on our end to protect it before it ships.

Since we do not upcharge for shipping, we do require the buyer to pay for return shipping. At this time we do not withhold a restocking charge, and refunds are equal to the sales price of the item only, at the time of purchase. Restocking fees may be introduced on a case-by-case basis only if we feel the system is being abused.

Exceptions to this are items that are manufacturer defective (no, that hand was not supposed to fall off Superman!) and fulfillment errors made by us (you received Donatello from TMNT instead of Squirtle). In those cases, we'll make it right.

Please start your return by emailing nubz@wildernessdealz.com and respond to any communication you receive. Have a Nub-riffic day!
