OUR STORY

About Nubz Toys & Collectibles

Nubz Toys & Collectibles was founded in 2026 with one simple mission: to just keep it simple. Remember when we used to just play? We owned the dialogue. We wrote the scenes. We defined the storyline — it didn't define us. Well, maybe it did, and we just got away from it for a while. From yesterday's nostalgia to discovering the vintage of tomorrow, we're here for it. Don't mind the fake airplane noises — we're just going on a little journey. Thanks for joining us. Remember, you might not be old enough for some toys & collectibles, but you're never too old for them.

Returns & Exchanges

You can return your orders of New items within 30 days of purchase provided it is unopened, you pay the shipping and the process here-in is followed Like-new/preowned merchandise is as-is & not refundable. For new items, please make sure the item you received is in unused and un-open condition with the tags and packaging intact. If you've opened the item, it cannot be returned. If the item was a clearance item, it cannot be returned. If the item is a seasonal/holiday themed item, it cannot be returned within 5 days before or at any point after that particular holiday.Start your return by emailing us at nubz@wildernessdealz.com. You will receive a response back with instructions and a form to submit. Upon receiving and inspecting your item, a refund will be processed within 10 business days according to the original method of payment. Customer is responsible for return shipping costs. We recommend using a trackable service; we are not liable for lost or delayed packages. A restocking and handling fee (typically 15%–30% of order value, subject to actual costs) will be deducted from your refund. Please note that original shipping charges are non-refundable, and all items must be returned in their original, unopened, and sealed packaging to be eligible for a refund
